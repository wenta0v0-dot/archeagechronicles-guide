ArcheAge Chronicles Launch-Day Guide — Your First Hours

Launch-day route for ArcheAge Chronicles: unlock times, first weapon, the Q weapon swap, level-25 build lock, and launch-week issues. Verified and dated.

10/31/2026 AAC Guide Last updated: 11/4/2026 4 min read

Quick Answer

ArcheAge Chronicles is live in Steam Early Access as of November 3, 2026 (23:16 UTC). This guide covers what to do in your first hours: the install checklist, picking one of the eight launch weapons, swapping with Q, the level-25 build lock, and the launch-week issues to know about — verified in-game and dated.

FAQ

When did ArcheAge Chronicles launch?
Early Access went live on November 3, 2026 at 23:16 UTC — 3 PM PST / 6 PM EST, and 7:16 AM on November 4 in Beijing and Singapore. It is buy-to-play at $29.99 with no subscription, and Early Access is expected to run 9–12 months before the full 1.0.
Which weapon should I pick first in ArcheAge Chronicles?
Each class has two launch weapons and both are viable: Warrior longsword or greatsword, Ranger hatchet or longbow, Mage elemental staff or wavesword, Berserker axe and shield or warhammer. Pick the playstyle you want to stare at for 30 hours — you can swap with Q mid-fight, but each weapon levels separately.
My ArcheAge Chronicles download will not start — what do I do?
On launch day several players reported the download not starting and the store page intermittently disappearing. Restarting Steam triggered the download for most of them. Try that first, then verify the game is in your library rather than the store page.
How does respeccing work in ArcheAge Chronicles?
Respeccing uses in-game currency. Each weapon levels its own skills and traits, so switching weapons is closer to starting a second build than editing your first — that separate progression is the real cost, not the currency.

ArcheAge Chronicles is live in Steam Early Access — it unlocked on November 3, 2026 at 23:16 UTC ($29.99, PC via Steam). The first hours still matter more here than in most MMOs, because the build economy makes your early weapon choice the decision that shapes the whole campaign. This route is verified against the live build, the Steam store page, and XLGames’ launch notices (November 4, 2026).

The launch-day priority

Test both weapons of your class, then commit. You swap with Q instantly and mid-fight, but each weapon levels its own skills and traits — so your second weapon is a second build, not a tweak. Try both while levels are cheap, then put everything into one before the mid-game.

Launch day: what actually happens

  • Unlock was 23:16 UTC on November 3 — 3 PM PST, 6 PM EST, and 7:16 AM on November 4 in Beijing and Singapore. That is the time XLGames’ “Early Access Is Now Live” notice went out.
  • Some players needed a Steam restart to start the download. On launch day the download failed to begin for part of the player base and the store page intermittently disappeared. If the install will not start, restart Steam and check your library first.
  • No server transfers, no cross-server play. XLGames confirmed that your character is locked to the server you create it on for the entirety of Early Access — and servers may lock as they fill, so the choice is effectively permanent for now.

Before you can play: the install checklist

  • Clear 100 GB on an SSD. That is the Steam page’s minimum storage requirement, alongside 16 GB RAM. Reports ahead of launch put 32 GB RAM at the recommended tier for 60 fps at 1080p — treat that as reported, not confirmed.
  • Claim the pre-order bonus in-game. Pre-purchase packages include the Dawn Pioneer Outfit Chest: create your character, then hold [Claim Reward] in the upper right of the main menu (or hold [N]), and use the chest from your inventory. It binds to the character that claims it (official Steam description, verified November 3, 2026).
  • Expect a queue-shaped first hour. Server population is the launch-day bottleneck, not your machine.

Hour 0: Pick your first weapon

Eight weapons, two per class. Both options are real builds — this is a taste test, not a tier list:

  • Warrior — longsword or greatsword. Parry timing and counters, with burst windows on the heavy swings. Longsword is the faster read; greatsword is the commitment.
  • Ranger — hatchet or longbow. Longbow is precision ranged damage against marked targets; the hatchet is a fast, lightning-charged close-range style. The class swaps between the two mid-fight better than any other.
  • Mage — elemental staff or wavesword. The staff is the area-of-effect caster with projectiles and meteor-style burst; the wavesword adds a melee cast for when things close the gap.
  • Berserker — axe and shield, or warhammer. Axe and shield is the pull-and-hold vanguard with the highest survivability; the warhammer trades safety for wide stagger and elemental burst, and every swing is a commitment.

Hours 1–10: the two decisions that matter

  1. Try your class’s other weapon before you invest. Press Q and the swap is instant, even mid-fight. The catch: the second weapon has its own skills and trait levels, so it starts behind. Test early, when “behind” costs an hour rather than a week.
  2. Watch the respec currency. Respeccing is a currency cost, not a free reset. From the mid-game onward, treat every respec as a planned decision.

The first week

  • Commit to one weapon by around level 25. That is the community checkpoint, carried over from pre-launch previews; this page will re-date it once the live build gives us real numbers.
  • Expect balance to move under you. XLGames shipped pre-launch tuning (wavesword, longbow, warhammer, elemental staff adjusted; Fire Wall nerfed; bosses more resistant to max-HP-scaling damage) and has a dedicated combat team working on stamina, hit reactions, movement speed, and attack interruption.
  • Do not plan around the Gladius yet. The Warrior’s third weapon arrives in the December update, not at launch.
