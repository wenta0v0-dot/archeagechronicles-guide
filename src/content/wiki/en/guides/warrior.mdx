The Warrior is ArcheAge Chronicles' **rhythm melee class**: read the enemy, parry on time, cash in counters for burst windows. It launches with two weapons — **longsword and greatsword** — a third (**Gladius**) officially dated for December, and a male-only gender lock until that same update. Verified against the live Early Access build and official material; community scores are attributed and dated below.

<Callout type="info" title="Who the Warrior is for">
  Players who like **timing-based melee** — the parry is the class. If you want to hold a shield line or kite from range, the [Berserker](/guides/berserker/) and [Ranger](/guides/ranger/) do that instead.
</Callout>

## The two launch weapons

| Weapon | PvE (community) | PvP (community) | The read |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Longsword** | 7 | **10** | The duelist's pick — worst live PvE score in the game, best PvP score in the game. |
| **Greatsword** | 9 | 9 | The balanced heavy — commit-heavy swings, steadier group output. |

Scores are community-sourced from AOEAH's launch-week tier list (November 4, 2026) — not measured by this site; see the [tier list](/guides/tier-list/) for the full table and its caveats.

**Longsword** is the specialist's weapon. Its value lives in the parry: time it, land the counter, and the duel swings. The 7/10 PvE score is the price of that identity — in group content the greatsword does the same job with less precision tax.

**Greatsword** trades some of that ceiling for consistency. Heavier swings, more commitment per action, and enough PvE output to carry the Warrior's group slot without leaning on the parry minigame.

## Community builds

From the same launch-week source (AOEAH, November 4, 2026):

- **PvE — Hybrid DPS**: Longsword + Greatsword. One kit covers the parry counters and the heavy cleave.
- **PvP — Duelist**: Longsword + Greatsword. The longsword duels, the greatsword punishes.

Remember the swap economy from the [classes guide](/guides/class-guide/): your second weapon levels its own skills and traits, so the pairing is an investment, not a toggle. Community guides report roughly **95 trait points per character with a 50–60 cap per weapon** (community-reported — verify against your own character panel).

## The Gladius changes the math in December

The Warrior is the first class to get a third weapon: the **Gladius**, officially dated for the **December update** — the same patch that unlocks the **female Warrior**. Community projections score it 9.5 PvE / 9 PvP (judged from known skills, not live play). If you are leveling a Warrior now, your longsword/greatsword traits carry over per the mastery rules — the Gladius starts fresh like any new weapon.

## Patch notes that touch the Warrior

- **November 7**: no Warrior-specific skill fixes in the maintenance notes — the trait changes (Warhammer stacking fix, Recommended Traits reset) hit Berserker builds, not this class.
- **Launch-week tuning** (pre-release): wavesword, longbow, warhammer, and elemental staff were adjusted; the Warrior kit went out as designed.

## How the Warrior compares

- Versus the [Berserker](/guides/berserker/): the Berserker out-tanks and anchors groups (10/10 PvE on Axe & Shield); the Warrior out-duels (10/10 PvP on Longsword).
- Versus the [Ranger](/guides/ranger/): the Ranger is the better solo farmer; the Warrior is the better PvP specialist.
- Versus the [Mage](/guides/mage/): the Mage rules area damage; the Warrior rules single-target rhythm.
