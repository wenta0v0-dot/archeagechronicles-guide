ArcheAge Chronicles launched on November 3, 2026, and XLGames posted its **[Key Issues on Early Access Launch Day and Our Response Plan](https://steamcommunity.com/app/3218230/allnews/)** on November 4 at 10:57 UTC, then shipped **two client patches** — one on November 4 (19:53 UTC) and a bigger one on November 7 (08:30 UTC). This page turns those notices into a fix sheet you can scan in a hurry. **Everything here is official-source material, last verified November 8, 2026** — no community guesswork.

<Callout type="warn" title="Update your client before retesting anything">
  The November 4 fixes only apply after the client updates. Open your **Steam Library** and let the update run; if no update button appears, **fully close and restart Steam** first. Several "still broken" reports after the maintenance were players on the pre-patch client.
</Callout>

## What shipped in the November 4 maintenance

Completed **November 4, 19:53 UTC** — apply via Steam Library (restart Steam if the update button does not appear):

- **Tutorial progression fix** — the launch-blocking quest bug is fixed.
- **Meliot spawn fix** — Meliot no longer fails to appear under the reported conditions.
- **Altar of Seals retry fix.**
- **Whisper display fix** — whispers sent by players now show.
- **Krogal fight hitbox improvements** across his attack patterns.
- **Four Diamond Shores quests are now abandonable**: Weeping Dead's Doll, Gnoll's Doll, Seaglass Beach Lantern's Calling, Watcher's Village Lantern's Calling.
- **Hereafter Stone can no longer be sold** (it can still be discarded), plus dismantle-setting adjustments and a fix for discarding Cheap Moonshine.
- Mutual attacks disabled at the Pioneers' Assembly Hall in the Conflict Channel.

## What shipped in the November 7 maintenance

Completed **November 7, 08:30 UTC** (started November 6, 23:00 PT) — the bigger of the two patches:

- **Cliffs of Madness un-blocked.** After defeating Poldat, you can now leave and **re-enter the area and speak with Kevin at the entrance** — the launch's most-reported quest dead-end.
- **Respawn system improved.** **Revive Nearby (F)** revives you at the nearest location regardless of Nui Statue or Nui Flame, and **Go Outside (G)** pulls you out of a Dimension if you died inside one.
- **Chat settings shipped.** Options now has chat settings: toggle the mini chat window on or off and choose which channels show in notifications — the long-requested "hide chat" option is live.
- **Storage weight raised from 200 to 1,000.**
- **Trait exploit closed.** The Recommended Traits exploit in Collapsed Southern Ruins is fixed, and **trait points it forcibly allocated have been reset** — some launch-week builds just lost their free points.
- **Combat fixes**: Reckless Destroyer effect, Ice Crystal duration, and the **Warhammer's Unstoppable Force II trait no longer stacks**; the Bauchu terrain cheese is gone.
- **Quest and channel fixes**: Sunken Glory, Diamond Shores Exploration, Weeping Dead's Doll, Gnoll's Doll, Phantoms of the Recalling Grounds, Bloodstained Note, duplicate rewards removed, Diamond Shores achievement counts corrected; Seaglass Tower and Malchuk channel entry/exit issues fixed; portal-shaped objects no longer have collision.

## Blocking progression

<Accordion title="Tutorial quest will not advance" open={true}>
  **Status: fixed — deployed in the November 4 maintenance.**

  The launch-blocking tutorial bug was patched on November 4 (19:53 UTC). If you are still stuck:

  1. Open your **Steam Library** and run the update.
  2. No update button? **Fully close and restart Steam**, then check again.
  3. Still blocked on the updated client? Report it in the official Discord bug channel — that means a new trigger condition, and it is no longer the launch bug.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Boss stopped spawning after you left the area">
  **Status: partially fixed.**

  In certain boss encounters, leaving the area and coming back can cause the boss to stop spawning permanently in that instance. The November 4 maintenance fixed a specific reported case — **Meliot failing to appear** — but the patch notes do not explicitly confirm the generic leave-and-return despawn fix that was announced as ready on November 4. Treat it as not fully shipped: **finish the encounter in one pull, and do not leave the arena mid-fight.** If you already triggered it, you may need to reset or re-enter the instance.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Malchuk encounter — boss will not appear, or you cannot respawn">
  **Status: under investigation — partially eased by the November 7 patch.**

  After dying during the Malchuk encounter, the boss may fail to appear again, and players may be unable to respawn. The November 7 patch fixed the related **Malchuk channel exit bug** and improved respawns globally (**Revive Nearby (F)** / **Go Outside (G)**), but the core spawn-after-death bug is not confirmed fixed. If it happens to you, post the details in the official Discord bug-report channel — reproduction reports are what they are asking for.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Quest progression breaks after an NPC conversation">
  **Status: the named case is fixed (November 7).**

  The most-reported case — **Cliffs of Madness** — now works: after defeating Poldat, leave and re-enter the area, then speak with **Kevin at the entrance**. The patch also fixed NPC interaction failures "under certain circumstances" and a list of named quest blockers (Sunken Glory, Diamond Shores Exploration, Weeping Dead's Doll, Gnoll's Doll, Phantoms of the Recalling Grounds, Bloodstained Note). Hit a dead-end in a different area? Report the NPC, the area, and the quest name.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Dungeon blocks after the party leader leaves or the party disbands">
  **Status: under investigation — a fix may take time.**

  XLGames calls this structural, which means it is not a quick patch. **The rule until then: do not disband and do not let the leader leave until every member has moved on to the next area after clearing the dungeon.**
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Dungeon boss missing with a party — the leader-swap trick (unofficial)">
  **Status: community-reported, not verified by this site.**

  A [Steam Discussions thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/3218230/discussions/0/571553910742881242) ("Missing dungeon boss FIX (Maybe)") reports this sequence working twice:

  1. **Transfer party leadership** to another member.
  2. Have **everyone leave the dungeon instance entirely**.
  3. **Re-enter** the dungeon.

  This is a player workaround for a bug XLGames has only partially patched (the Meliot case, November 4). Treat it as a lottery ticket, not a fix — and the official rule still applies: do not leave a boss arena mid-fight.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Party members cannot pick up dungeon keys">
  **Status: known behaviour, not a bug fix.**

  Key items are **not shared** across the party. Each member has to collect their own key. If one player is short a key, that is why.
</Accordion>

## Getting unstuck

<Accordion title="Character stuck in the terrain" open={true}>
  **Status: workaround available now.**

  1. Press **ESC**, then select **Escape**.
  2. If that does not work, **restart the game**, log back in, and use **Escape** once more.

  That is the official sequence. If it still fails, report the location in the official Discord bug channel.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Game hangs during anti-cheat initialization">
  **Status: handle by ticket.**

  The game can stall while the anti-cheat initializes. Take a **screenshot of the screen where it hangs** and submit a ticket through Customer Support — reachable from the in-game menu with **ESC**. XLGames reviews these case by case, so the screenshot matters.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Unexpected crashes">
  **Status: partially addressed in the next patch.**

  XLGames says some crash reports are handled in the next patch and that it will keep investigating new ones. If a crash is reproducible, the Discord bug channel is the fastest route.
</Accordion>

## Launch, black screen, and crash fixes

Collected from community guides (MetaForge, November 4; LagZapper) and standard Steam troubleshooting. Work down the list and **relaunch after each step that applies**:

1. **Wait out the first launch.** A black screen for up to **~10 minutes** on first boot is usually shader compilation — do not force-restart while the disk/CPU is still working.
2. **Restart Steam fully.** Steam → Exit, then end any leftover game or Steam processes in **Task Manager**, and relaunch. (This is also the official tip for a missing update button after maintenance.)
3. **Disable overlays.** Turn off the in-game Steam Overlay (Settings → In Game) and any third-party overlays (Discord, MSI Afterburner, renderer hooks).
4. **Verify game files.** Library → right-click the game → Properties → Installed Files → **Verify integrity of game files**.
5. **Check your antivirus for XIGNCODE3 quarantine.** The game uses the XIGNCODE3 anti-cheat; security software can silently quarantine its files. Restore/exclude rather than delete.
6. **GPU selection and drivers.** Set the game to High performance in Windows Graphics settings, and update drivers (NVIDIA App / AMD Adrenalin / Intel DSA).
7. **Intel Core Ultra NPU crash workaround** (player-reported, via MetaForge): disable **Intel AI Boost** under Neural processors in Device Manager, launch the game, then re-enable it.

<Callout type="tip" title="Launch problems that are actually the anti-cheat">
  Stuck on a **"preparing security module"**-style screen is the anti-cheat initializer, not a broken install — no reinstall will fix it. Screenshot the exact screen and open a support ticket (ESC → Customer Support).
</Callout>

## Settings worth changing now

<Accordion title="Chat is full of languages you do not read">
  As of the **November 7 patch**, chat settings live in **Options**: toggle the mini chat window on or off and choose which channels appear in notifications. The language filter remains at **Settings → Game Options → Chat Language Filter**.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="You cannot find your party in the same channel">
  There is a **channel transfer button in the party UI** that moves you to the party leader's channel — but it only appears under certain conditions, which is the actual complaint. XLGames plans to widen the conditions and improve the guidance. Until then, check the party frame before assuming you are on the wrong channel.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="You want to rebind movement keys">
  Free movement-key rebinding is **not possible yet** — XLGames has acknowledged the request and plans to make movement keys freely reassignable, no date given. Meanwhile you **can** rebind around 10 UI shortcuts and invert the camera Y-axis separately for mouse and controller.
</Accordion>

## Maintenance and platform limits

- **Two client patches are live: November 4** (19:53 UTC) **and November 7** (08:30 UTC). Contents listed above. Future maintenance windows get announced through the Steam news hub, and the schedule can shift.
- **Linux and Steam Deck are not officially supported.** No timeline for support — and community Proton/file-modification workarounds carry **account risk** under the anti-cheat; XLGames discourages them.
- **Steam Family Sharing is intentionally unsupported** — one purchase, one library.
- **Optimization is ongoing.** Stuttering and frame-rate drops are widespread and environment-dependent, so there is no single fix yet.
- **Enforcement has started.** On November 5, XLGames announced sanctions against accounts using system exploits, and says monitoring continues with further bans to be announced through the same notice channel. Do not use third-party tools or exploits — the first ban wave already landed.

<Callout type="tip" title="Where to report a bug">
  XLGames is collecting reports from three places: the **official Discord bug-report channel**, **Steam Discussions**, and **Customer Support** (ESC menu → Customer Support, or the ticket portal). Include the quest or NPC name, your server, and what you did immediately before the break — those are the details they say they need.
</Callout>
