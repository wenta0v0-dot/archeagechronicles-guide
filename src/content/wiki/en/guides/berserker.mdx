The Berserker is ArcheAge Chronicles' **frontline class** — "Break Through. Never Break," per the official class intro. It launches with **Axe and Shield plus Warhammer**, a male-only gender lock, and the single best PvE weapon score in the game. Verified against the live Early Access build; community scores and skill names attributed below.

<Callout type="info" title="Who the Berserker is for">
  Players who want to **hold the line** — charge in, absorb, pull packs into position, and punish. If you want to kite or out-duel on timing, the [Ranger](/guides/ranger/) and [Warrior](/guides/warrior/) do that instead.
</Callout>

## The two launch weapons

| Weapon | PvE (community) | PvP (community) | The read |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Axe & Shield** | **10** | 9 | The best PvE weapon in the game — the aggressive vanguard tank. |
| **Warhammer** | 5 | 5 | The elemental bruiser — rated last, but its stacking bug was fixed November 7. |

Scores are community-sourced from AOEAH's launch-week tier list (November 4, 2026) — not measured by this site; see the [tier list](/guides/tier-list/) for the full table and its caveats.

**Axe and Shield** is the reason group content wants a Berserker: charge-in tanking with pull-and-hold control and steady cleave damage. Community class guides (reporting the official skill reveals) name its toolkit: **Reckless Destroyer** (axe strikes that build the enemy bleed meter), **Shield Slam** (bash with spirit return), **Reel In** (chain-pull to close distance), and **Brutal Crash** (a lunge through enemy lines with stagger and damage reduction). Skill names as reported — verify in-game.

**Warhammer** is the high-risk half: slow recovery frames, every swing a commitment, fire-and-lightning burst built through a stored-energy loop (community guides name **Surging Heat**, **Meteor Hammer**, **Galactic Impact**, **Resonating Strike**, and **Thundering Hurl**). Its 5/5 score was measured **while its Unstoppable Force II trait had a stacking bug** — fixed in the November 7 maintenance. Re-test before treating it as trash; the design's stagger-first-then-burst loop is the same, but the trait math changed.

## Community builds

From the same launch-week source (AOEAH, November 4, 2026):

- **PvE — Main Tank**: Axe & Shield (upgrade path: Great Shield when it ships — projected, not confirmed).
- **PvE — Solo Boss Killer**: Axe & Shield + Warhammer.
- **PvP — Line-Breaker**: Axe & Shield + Warhammer.

Community guides also call Axe & Shield the **best beginner-friendly pick in the game** — highest survivability, forgiving mistakes. The swap economy applies: the Warhammer levels its own skills and traits, so the pairing is an investment. Community guides report roughly **95 trait points per character with a 50–60 cap per weapon** (community-reported — verify against your own panel).

## Patch notes that touch the Berserker

- **November 17 (announced November 13): the Axe was reviewed but deferred.** Design lead Jay Lee's balance letter says Warrior Greatsword and Berserker Axe were examined alongside the Mage and Ranger changes, but XLGames chose to ship the urgent fixes first and adjust other weapons step by step — no Berserker change on November 17 yet.
- **November 12**: **Warhammer's Pulverize 3-hit combo got a clearer visual effect area** (readability fix, not a damage change). Raid bosses also gained significant elemental resistance — tank relevance rises as burst-damage cheese dies. Plus the standard client fixes (storage, login with certain characters).
- **November 7**: **Warhammer's Unstoppable Force II trait no longer stacks** — the direct fix behind the weapon's re-test case. The Recommended Traits exploit in Collapsed Southern Ruins was also closed, with forcibly allocated trait points reset.
- **November 4**: hitbox detection improvements across the **Krogal** fight's attack patterns (a boss encounter, not a class change — but relevant if you tank him).
- **Launch-week tuning** (pre-release): the **warhammer was among the weapons adjusted** before release.

## How the Berserker compares

- Versus the [Warrior](/guides/warrior/): the Berserker anchors groups and out-tanks everything; the Warrior out-duels.
- Versus the [Mage](/guides/mage/): the tank and the artillery — the pairing most group builds are built around.
- Versus the [Ranger](/guides/ranger/): opposite problems — the Berserker never leaves the pack's face; the Ranger never stands still.
