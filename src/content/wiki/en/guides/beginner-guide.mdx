ArcheAge Chronicles goes live in **Early Access on November 3, 2026** ($29.99, PC via Steam), and the first hours matter more here than in most MMOs — because the game's build economy makes your **level-25 lock-in** the decision that shapes the whole campaign. This launch-day guide walks the route: verified against official sources from XLGames and the Steam store page, dated November 2026.

<Callout type="info" title="The launch-day priority">
  **Experiment early, commit by 25.** Weapon skills are cheap to try and expensive to re-level. Your first hours should be spent tasting two or three weapons — then locking the one whose combat flow you enjoy, before the respec economy starts biting.
</Callout>

## Unlock times: November 3, 3 PM PST

The Steam unlock hits **3 PM PST / 6 PM EST / 11 PM UTC on November 3** — the morning of **November 4 (7 AM) in East Asia**. Verified against the Steam page countdown and XLGames' launch announcement (November 3, 2026).

## Before you can play: the install checklist

- **Clear 100 GB on an SSD.** The Steam page lists 100 GB of storage on an SSD and 16 GB RAM as minimum requirements — that is a big install, so start the download as early as Steam allows.
- **Claim the pre-order bonus in-game.** Pre-purchase packages include the **Dawn Pioneer Outfit Chest**: create your character, then hold **[Claim Reward]** in the upper-right of the main menu (or hold **[N]**), and use the chest from your inventory. It binds to the character that claims it (official Steam description, verified November 3, 2026).
- **Three server regions at EA start.** XLGames confirmed three server regions for Early Access, with server details published alongside its pre-launch producer letter in late October — choose based on your ping once you see the options in-game.

## Hour 0: Pick your first weapon

The four launch classes, by feel:

- **Warrior** (longsword, greatsword, gladius) — parry timing and counters; the pick for players who like rhythm-based melee with burst windows.
- **Ranger** (hatchet, longbow, crossbow) — mobility and distance; kiting enemies with ranged shots or quick melee strikes.
- **Mage** (elemental staff, wave sword) — area-of-effect elemental spells, projectiles, meteor summons; the pick for players who like positioning and spell rosters.
- **Berserker** (axe and shield, great axe) — tanky brawler with crowd control and stagger; the pick for players who like to absorb and punish.

## Hours 1–10: The two decisions that matter

1. **Test a second weapon before level 25.** Switching retains the original skill's level, so your first experiment is nearly free — but the *new* skill re-levels through use. The earlier you test, the cheaper the experiment.
2. **Watch the respec currency.** Respeccing is a currency cost, not a free reset. From level 25 onward, treat every respec as a planned decision, not a whim.

## The first week

- **Follow the main progression to the level-25 lock** — it is the community-recommended checkpoint for committing to your build layout.
