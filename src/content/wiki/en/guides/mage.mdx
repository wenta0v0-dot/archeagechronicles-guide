The Mage is ArcheAge Chronicles' **area-damage class**: spell rosters, positioning, and screens full of effects. It launches with **Elemental Staff and Wavesword**, a female-only gender lock, and the strongest zone-control resume in the game. Verified against the live Early Access build and official class material; community scores attributed and dated below.

<Callout type="info" title="Who the Mage is for">
  Players who like **positioning games and spell rosters** — picking the spell for the pack, not the parry timing. If you want to hold a front line or play the ranged kiting duel, the [Berserker](/guides/berserker/) and [Ranger](/guides/ranger/) do that instead.
</Callout>

## The two launch weapons

| Weapon | PvE (community) | PvP (community) | The read |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Elemental Staff** | **9** | **9.5** | The engine — AoE spells, projectiles, meteor-style burst. |
| **Wavesword** | 8 | 9 | The melee cast for when things close the gap. |

Scores are community-sourced from AOEAH's launch-week tier list (November 4, 2026) — not measured by this site; see the [tier list](/guides/tier-list/) for the full table and its caveats.

**Elemental Staff** carries the class: area-of-effect elemental spells and projectiles with high-power finishes like the official-intro meteor summon. In group content it is the AoE clear; in PvP it is the zone control tool that the community rates above everything except the longsword's dueling.

**Wavesword** is the survival answer. Melee-range casting that punishes people for diving a "squishy" — and the reason the community's Spellblade Assassin build exists. Like every second weapon, it levels its own skills and traits, so the pairing is an investment.

## Community builds

From the same launch-week source (AOEAH, November 4, 2026):

- **PvE — AoE Clear**: Elemental Staff + Wavesword.
- **PvP — Spellblade Assassin**: Wavesword + Elemental Staff.
- **PvP — Zone Controller**: Elemental Staff + Wavesword.

Community guides report roughly **95 trait points per character with a 50–60 cap per weapon** (community-reported — verify against your own character panel).

## Patch notes that touch the Mage

- **November 7**: the maintenance notes list a **duration fix for the Ice Crystal skill effect** (the notes name the skill, not the class — confirm on your own panel after updating). No other Mage-specific fixes.
- **Launch-week tuning** (pre-release): the **elemental staff was among the weapons adjusted** before release; the current scores reflect that pass.

## How the Mage compares

- Versus the [Ranger](/guides/ranger/): the Ranger kites safer and farms solo better; the Mage clears areas faster and controls zones harder.
- Versus the [Warrior](/guides/warrior/): the Warrior wins the single-target duel; the Mage wins the battlefield.
- Versus the [Berserker](/guides/berserker/): the tank and the artillery — every top-end group wants one of each.
