## Quick picks by playstyle

Community build picks from the same source (AOEAH, November 4, 2026):

**PvE**

- **Main tank** — Axe & Shield (upgrade path: Great Shield when it ships)
- **Solo boss killer** — Axe & Shield + Warhammer *(re-test Warhammer after the November 7 trait fix)*
- **AoE clear** — Elemental Staff + Wave Sword
- **Open-world farming** — Longbow + Hatchet
- **Hybrid DPS** — Longsword + Greatsword

**PvP**

- **Duelist** — Longsword + Greatsword
- **Spellblade assassin** — Wave Sword + Elemental Staff
- **Skirmisher** — Hatchet + Longbow
- **Line-breaker** — Axe & Shield + Warhammer

Remember the swap rule from the [classes guide](/guides/class-guide/): your second weapon is a second build with its own trait levels, so the pairings above are real investments, not loadout swaps.

## Why the bottom of this list may move first

- **Warhammer** was rated with its stacking bug live; the fix shipped November 7, and its Pulverize combo got a visual-area improvement on November 12.
- **Combat feel** (stamina, hit reactions, movement speed, attack interruption) is under active rework by XLGames' dedicated combat team — a feel patch can re-rank every weapon at once.
- **The trait-point exploit is dead.** The November 7 maintenance fixed the Recommended Traits exploit in Collapsed Southern Ruins and **reset the trait points it had forcibly allocated** — some launch-week "S-tier" builds were running free points and are now built like everyone else.

## The November 17 balance patch — LIVE, with exact numbers

Design lead Jay Lee's November 13 letter promised the changes for Tuesday, November 17, and the scheduled maintenance that day shipped them. These are the patch-note numbers — **every score above is now pre-patch data**:

**Elemental Staff (nerfed):**

- **Wall of Fire**: activation + damage-over-time **−30%**, duration 8 → 6 seconds
- **Flamethrower** damage **−15%**
- **Hellfire** additional-hit damage **−60%** — the "one skill doing two jobs" combo is broken up
- **Shooting Star** damage **+100%**, SP cost 530 → 470 — a real compensation buff, not a token one

**Longbow (buffed):**

- Weapon Physical/Magic ATK **+7.7%**
- **Focus Fire, Fire Arrow, Scarlet Arrow** +60% (+72% including the weapon increase)
- All Maneuver skills (Abdominal Kick, Flip Kick, Arrow Stab, Counterattack Maneuver, Crescent Kick) +40% (+51% including weapon)

**Systemic:** Attribute DEF now reduces status-effect buildup **and** triggered damage — the anti-fire-mage counterstat went live the same day.

**Also shipped November 17:** processed-material recipes no longer need Common/Uncommon loot (Ingots, Lumber, Leather, Textile, Archeum, Additive, Raw Meat), shop sell prices dropped economy-wide (the economy letter followed November 20), Lunastone dismantling added, the party no longer disbands when its leader leaves a channel, and Longsword's Triple Slash lost its final-hit hitstop.

**How to act on this:** the Mage and Ranger rows above are pre-patch numbers kept only as the dated snapshot. The official numbers say the staff's spam skills came down hard while Shooting Star doubled; the longbow finally pays out. Re-rank after the community absorbs the patch — which is exactly what Hard Mode (Celtak/Crimson Rift/Brutal Nightmare, live November 20) and the December Azhra raid will test for real.

## One more caveat: temper data was mis-linked at launch

The November 12 maintenance fixed incorrectly linked **Temper** data on certain weapons and armor — some gear had gained far more stats than intended, others far less (design lead Jay Lee's [November 11 apology letter](https://steamcommunity.com/app/3218230/allnews/) explains the fix-without-nerf approach: base stats raised and a new bonus stat added rather than reverting, which would have cut some weapons' ATK by 30%). **Launch-week community scores were measured partly on mis-tempered gear.** Raid bosses also gained significant elemental resistance on November 12 — the "bosses die too fast" era is over.

## When this list changes

- **November 17**: the announced balance patch lands (staff nerf, longbow buff). This page re-dates once the community re-ranks — the projected-weapon rows also start resolving if the December Gladius slips forward.
- **Hard Mode: Celtak and the Crimson Rift** land the same week (per the official preview video): much stronger bosses, a respawn delay, and **Epic Lunastones** as rewards — the first content that actually demands group optimization.
- **December update**: the **Gladius** lands officially, the **female Warrior** arrives (existing characters can switch gender with a free Customization Ticket), and the **Azhra raid** opens — its materials craft **Legendary weapons and light armor**, the first gear tier above current crafting (all per Jay Lee's official preview video, November 12). Azhra gives the PvE rows their first raid-scale test.
- Every re-check gets a new date on this page. If the live meta contradicts the snapshot above, the contradiction wins.
