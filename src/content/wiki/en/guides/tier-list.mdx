## Quick picks by playstyle

Community build picks from the same source (AOEAH, November 4, 2026):

**PvE**

- **Main tank** — Axe & Shield (upgrade path: Great Shield when it ships)
- **Solo boss killer** — Axe & Shield + Warhammer *(re-test Warhammer after the November 7 trait fix)*
- **AoE clear** — Elemental Staff + Wave Sword
- **Open-world farming** — Longbow + Hatchet
- **Hybrid DPS** — Longsword + Greatsword

**PvP**

- **Duelist** — Longsword + Greatsword
- **Spellblade assassin** — Wave Sword + Elemental Staff
- **Skirmisher** — Hatchet + Longbow
- **Line-breaker** — Axe & Shield + Warhammer

Remember the swap rule from the [classes guide](/guides/class-guide/): your second weapon is a second build with its own trait levels, so the pairings above are real investments, not loadout swaps.

## Why the bottom of this list may move first

- **Warhammer** was rated with its stacking bug live; the fix shipped November 7, and its Pulverize combo got a visual-area improvement on November 12.
- **Combat feel** (stamina, hit reactions, movement speed, attack interruption) is under active rework by XLGames' dedicated combat team — a feel patch can re-rank every weapon at once.
- **The trait-point exploit is dead.** The November 7 maintenance fixed the Recommended Traits exploit in Collapsed Southern Ruins and **reset the trait points it had forcibly allocated** — some launch-week "S-tier" builds were running free points and are now built like everyone else.

## The November 17 balance patch is announced — the top of the list moves too

Design lead Jay Lee published **[Jay's Notes — Class and Crafting Balance Improvements](https://steamcommunity.com/app/3218230/allnews/)** on November 13, previewing the Tuesday, November 17 update. It directly hits the top and middle of this table:

- **Elemental Staff (9 / 9.5) is getting nerfed.** Fire attack skills drop — **Wall of Fire** and **Flamethrower** specifically (the latter for stacking high damage with efficient status application via the Hellfire trait). In return, **Shooting Star** gets stronger. XLGames says it simulated scenarios with top players' gear before deciding.
- **Longbow (8 / 7.5) is getting a major buff.** Weapon ATK increases for all longbows; **Focus Fire, Fire Arrow, and Scarlet Arrow** power is "greatly increased"; all **Maneuver** skills are greatly increased. Rapid Fire and Aimed Shot keep their skill power but gain from the weapon-ATK rise.
- **Elemental DEF starts protecting against status effects** — both buildup and damage, with a floor so effects can never be fully ignored. This softens the fire-mage damage model against geared opponents.
- **Greatsword (Warrior) and Axe (Berserker) were reviewed but deferred** — XLGames chose to ship the urgent fixes now and adjust other weapons step by step.
- **Crafting recipes get simplified** the same day: monster loot and Sweetgrass Powder are removed from processed-material recipes (Ingots, Textiles, Additives).

**How to act on this:** the Mage and Ranger rows above are pre-patch numbers. If you are committing a weapon this week, the community direction of travel is: staff mains should expect a smaller damage ceiling, longbow mains should expect their investment to finally pay out. The scores on this page get re-dated after the patch lands and the community re-ranks — not before.

## One more caveat: temper data was mis-linked at launch

The November 12 maintenance fixed incorrectly linked **Temper** data on certain weapons and armor — some gear had gained far more stats than intended, others far less (design lead Jay Lee's [November 11 apology letter](https://steamcommunity.com/app/3218230/allnews/) explains the fix-without-nerf approach: base stats raised and a new bonus stat added rather than reverting, which would have cut some weapons' ATK by 30%). **Launch-week community scores were measured partly on mis-tempered gear.** Raid bosses also gained significant elemental resistance on November 12 — the "bosses die too fast" era is over.

## When this list changes
