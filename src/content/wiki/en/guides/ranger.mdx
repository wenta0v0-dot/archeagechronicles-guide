The Ranger is ArcheAge Chronicles' **mobility class** — the one kit that never has to stop moving, switching between lightning-charged melee and precision ranged fire. It launches with **Hatchet and Longbow**, a female-only gender lock, and the best solo-farming resume in the game. Verified against the live Early Access build; community scores attributed and dated below.

<Callout type="info" title="Who the Ranger is for">
  Players who want **one character that does everything alone** — farm, kite, duel, and never wait on a group. If you want to anchor a tank line or duel on timing, the [Berserker](/guides/berserker/) and [Warrior](/guides/warrior/) do that instead.
</Callout>

## The two launch weapons

| Weapon | PvE (community) | PvP (community) | The read |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hatchet** | **9.5** | **9.5** | The best all-rounder in the game — fast, lightning-charged melee. |
| **Longbow** | 8 | 7.5 | Precision ranged damage against marked targets. |

Scores are community-sourced from AOEAH's launch-week tier list (November 4, 2026) — not measured by this site; see the [tier list](/guides/tier-list/) for the full table and its caveats.

**Hatchet** is the headline. Community guides describe a fast, close-range "savage hunter" style with lightning effects, and it is the only weapon scoring 9.5 in both columns — it farms, it duels, it fills groups. If a new player asks which single weapon to ride to level 25, the community answer is this one.

**Longbow** is the control half of the kit: mark targets, punish from range, and farm open-world packs without taking hits. Its group PvE score trails the Hatchet, which is why the community pairing uses both rather than committing to one.

## Community builds

From the same launch-week source (AOEAH, November 4, 2026):

- **PvE — Open-World Farmer**: Longbow + Hatchet. Kite packs down at range, finish with melee.
- **PvP — Skirmisher**: Hatchet + Longbow. Dive with the Hatchet, disengage and re-mark with the bow.

The swap economy applies: the second weapon levels its own skills and traits, so the pairing is an investment. Community guides report roughly **95 trait points per character with a 50–60 cap per weapon** (community-reported — verify against your own character panel).

## Patch notes that touch the Ranger

- **November 7**: no Ranger-specific skill fixes in the maintenance notes.
- **Launch-week tuning** (pre-release): the **longbow was among the weapons adjusted** before release — its current scores already reflect that pass.

## How the Ranger compares

- Versus the [Mage](/guides/mage/): both kite, but the Mage trades safety for area damage; the Ranger trades burst for self-sufficiency.
- Versus the [Warrior](/guides/warrior/): the Warrior is the PvP specialist; the Ranger is the PvE-all-rounder with a Hatchet that duels anyway.
- Versus the [Berserker](/guides/berserker/): opposite problems — the Berserker holds ground, the Ranger never stands still.
