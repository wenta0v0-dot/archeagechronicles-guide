ArcheAge Chronicles launched into **Early Access on November 3, 2026** ($29.99, PC via Steam), and its class system works differently from most MMOs: **four classes, eight weapons, two per class** — and each weapon carries its own skill set, so switching changes your whole combat flow. This guide maps all four classes, the eight launch weapons, the gender locks, and what the December update adds — verified in-game and against XLGames' launch material (November 4, 2026).

<Callout type="info" title="The one rule to remember">
  You **swap weapons with Q**, instantly, even mid-fight — but each weapon levels its own skills and traits. Your second weapon is a **separate build**, not a modifier. That is why the community advice is to try both early and commit to one main weapon before the mid-game.
</Callout>

## The four launch classes

Verified against the live Early Access build and XLGames' class material (November 4, 2026):

| Class | Launch weapons | Gender | Playstyle |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Warrior** | Longsword, Greatsword | Male only | Parry timing, counters, and high-damage burst windows. |
| **Ranger** | Hatchet, Longbow | Female only | Mobility and distance — kiting on the longbow, fast lightning-charged melee on the hatchet. |
| **Mage** | Elemental Staff, Wavesword | Female only | Area-of-effect elemental spells and projectiles on the staff; the wavesword adds a melee cast. |
| **Berserker** | Axe and Shield, Warhammer | Male only | Axe and shield is the tanky, pull-and-hold vanguard; the warhammer trades safety for wide stagger and elemental burst. |

That is **eight weapons at launch**, two per class — not three. Earlier third-weapon options are post-launch additions, listed below.

## How weapon mastery works

Three rules govern your build in the launch build:

1. **Your weapon defines your kit.** Skills and combat flow are bound to the weapon — the class boundary *is* the weapon boundary.
2. **Q swaps instantly.** You can change weapons mid-fight; the swap is not a menu action.
3. **Each weapon levels separately.** Traits and skills are tied to the weapon, not the class — so two Warriors on different weapons play entirely different games.

## Gender locks

Every class is gender-locked at launch: **Warrior and Berserker are male-only; Ranger and Mage are female-only**. XLGames has said it intends to remove gender locks from every class by full release, one class at a time. This was the most common complaint from the September network test, and it is confirmed as the first item on the post-launch schedule.

## What the December update adds

XLGames' stated plan is a roughly monthly update cadence during Early Access:

- **Female Warrior** — the first class to lose its gender lock.
- **Gladius** — the Warrior's third weapon (a short sword), arriving alongside the female Warrior option.

Neither is in the launch build. Do not plan a Gladius build for launch week.

## When to lock your build

Pre-launch previews and community guidance converge on the same checkpoint: **lock in your preferred weapon by around level 25**. Before that, experiment freely — respeccing is a currency cost, not a permanent mistake. After that, re-leveling a swapped weapon line through use becomes the real price, so decide your main weapon, then commit.

## Launch-week status

- **Combat is the live controversy.** Players are split on stamina, hit reactions, movement speed, and attack interruption. XLGames has acknowledged the division and assigned a dedicated combat team to keep refining it. Expect balance to move under you.
- **Weapon tuning already happened at launch.** The wavesword, longbow, warhammer, and elemental staff were adjusted before release; Fire Wall was nerfed, and bosses are now more resistant to max-HP-scaling damage.
- **No server transfers, no cross-server play.** Your character is locked to the server you create it on for all of Early Access.
