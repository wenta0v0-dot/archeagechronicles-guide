ArcheAge Chronicles enters **Early Access on November 3, 2026** ($29.99, PC via Steam — unlock at 3 PM PST / 6 PM EST / 11 PM UTC), and its class system works differently from most MMOs: there are **four launch classes, and each is defined entirely by the weapon you hold**. Switch weapons and your skills, combat flow and role change with it. This guide maps all four launch classes, the weapon mastery rules, and the one respec decision that matters most — verified against the official class introductions from XLGames (October 2026).

<Callout type="info" title="The one rule to remember">
  Your armament **completely changes your skills and combat flow** — and respeccing **costs in-game currency**. The community advice: try weapons early, then **lock in your preferred build layout by around level 25**, because re-leveling swapped skills through use is the expensive part.
</Callout>

## The four launch classes

Verified against the official class introductions (XLGames, July–September 2026):

| Class | Weapons | Playstyle |
|---|---|---|
| **Warrior** | Longsword, Greatsword, Gladius | Parry timing, counters, and high-damage burst windows. |
| **Ranger** | Hatchet, Longbow, Crossbow | Mobility and distance — kite enemies with ranged shots or quick melee strikes. |
| **Mage** | Elemental Staff, Wave Sword | Area-of-effect elemental spells, projectiles, and high-power abilities like meteor summons. |
| **Berserker** | Axe and Shield, Great Axe | Tanky brawler with high resilience and heavy crowd-control or stagger output. |

## How weapon mastery works

Three verified rules govern your build:

1. **Your armament defines your kit.** Skills and combat flow are bound to the weapon — the class boundary *is* the weapon boundary.
2. **Switching keeps what you earned.** Changing active abilities retains the level of the original skill — you never lose progress outright.
3. **New skills start lower.** Freshly selected skills must be re-leveled through use. This is the cost that makes late respecs painful.

## When to lock your build

The community consensus from the launch window: **lock in your preferred build layout by around level 25**. Before that, experiment freely — the respec cost is currency, not souls. After that, re-leveling a swapped skill line through use becomes the real price, so decide your main weapon, then commit.
